# Part 3 — Outcome Assignment

## Title
**My AI-Powered Engineering Playbook**

## Purpose
Prove you can use AI as a development assistant — not a blind copy machine.

## Deliverable (900–1300 words, Markdown/PDF)

### Section 1 — Foundations brief
Explain in your own words (short paragraphs):

* AI vs Generative AI vs LLM  
* tokens  
* context / context window  
* how pricing generally works (subscription vs usage)

### Section 2 — Tool map
For **Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity**:

* what it is;  
* when you might use it;  
* one caution.

(Based on class demos + learning notes.)

### Section 3 — Industry lessons
Summarize Uber and Microsoft cases and extract 4 rules for a future team budget/policy.

### Section 4 — Spec + prompt lab (product-linked)
Take **one must-have feature** from your Module 3 MVP and provide:

1. mini-spec (objective, user, constraints, acceptance criteria);  
2. weak prompt;  
3. strong prompt;  
4. AI output summary (run it if possible);  
5. your review notes (what you accept/reject and why);  
6. how you verified (or would verify).

### Section 5 — Personal responsible AI policy
8–10 lines covering secrets, verification, no-AI assessments, honesty, and cost awareness.

## Constraints
* Focus on software engineering use, not generic chatbot entertainment.  
* Do not include real secrets.  
* Disclose AI help used to polish writing.  
* If a tool wasn’t available on your machine, write observation from teacher demo honestly.

## Rubric (100)

| Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|
| Correct foundations (tokens/context/models/pricing) | 20 |
| Accurate tool understanding | 15 |
| Quality lessons from Uber/Microsoft cases | 15 |
| Spec + strong prompt + critical review | 30 |
| Responsible AI policy seriousness | 15 |
| Clarity | 5 |

**Pass:** 70+
